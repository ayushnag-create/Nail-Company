About KHUSHÉ

Beauty that fits your life.

KHUSHÉ began with a simple wish: salon-perfect nails without the two-hour appointment. “Khushi” means happiness — and that’s what we want every set to feel like.

Each design is hand-finished in small batches using soft, flexible gel that looks natural and stays put. Press them on in minutes, wear them for weeks, then take them off and wear them again.
